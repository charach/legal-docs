You can delete your **VocalWarm** account and all associated data directly from the app, or by contacting us via email. VocalWarm is operated by **TtomStudio**.

## Option 1: Delete from the App

1. Open **VocalWarm** and sign in.
2. Go to **Settings** (bottom navigation bar).
3. Scroll to the bottom and tap **"Delete Account & Data"**.
4. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

## Option 2: Request via Email

If you cannot access the app, send an email to [official@ttomstudio.com](mailto:official@ttomstudio.com) from the email address associated with your account. Include **"Account Deletion Request"** in the subject line. We will process your request within 7 business days.

## What Gets Deleted

- Your Firebase Authentication account (email, display name)
- All Firestore documents under your user ID:
    - Practice sessions
    - Vocal range test results
    - Note accuracy records
- Local app data (Room database) on the current device

**This action is permanent and cannot be undone.**

## Data Retention

After deletion, no personal data is retained. Anonymized, aggregated analytics data (e.g., crash reports collected by Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics) may persist but cannot be linked back to your account.
